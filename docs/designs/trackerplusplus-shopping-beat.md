## Problem Statement

Today's Personal Agents Hackathon brief is "Build a shopping agent with an audit trail": help someone make a purchase or complete a shopping task, use reliable context to make decisions, take a meaningful action, and show the information and steps behind what it did. TrackerPlusPlus plans and defends workouts but buys nothing. Its strengths already match the second half of the brief: the action feed (verb, before → after, reason, timestamp, Undo), real calendar actions, notifications, a Live Activity and the gym geofence. Submissions close at 4:30 PM PT; finalist demos start at 5:00 PM.

## What Makes This Cool

The agent buys things because the plan needs them, not because someone asked. Every purchase traces back to a rule the person set once, cites the facts it used, names the options it rejected and why, and keeps watching afterwards: it cancels its own day pass when the forecast clears, or closes the trail with "Arrived Demo Gym 6:34 PM · pass used" from the gym geofence. Most shopping-agent demos end at checkout; this one shows the purchase changing the plan (break-in runs land on the calendar) and the agent reversing its own decision when the reason goes away.

## Premises

1. **No real money moves.** Checkout is a clearly labeled demo merchant ("Demo store, no charge", "Demo Gym (no charge)"). The context, rule, options and trail are real.
2. **A standing spending mandate.** The person sets it once: "Ask me before anything over $20. Day passes up to $20: book it, tell me after." Under-$20 day passes are bought automatically with a cancel window (the existing Undo); anything else waits for one tap. The mandate is the first line of every purchase's trail.
3. **The audit trail lives in the feed.** Purchases use the same five-part card and feed row as every action, plus one new "Why" screen: rule, what it noticed, what it checked (cited passages), options and why each lost, what it did, outcome. No separate shopping area.
4. **Senso runs on the harness, live, with a cached fallback (revised in session).** The harness queries Senso during the demo; the phone ships the cached answers so the stand-in agent and an offline stage still work. The Why screen marks each citation set "live" or "cached". (Originally cache-only; revised after the second opinion pointed out that Senso's judges would never see Senso run.)
5. **Triggers are demo controls.** "Rain at 6:30 PM", "Forecast clears" and "Jordan: shoes near 400 mi" rather than a live weather API or a real mileage history.

### Contract (Swift `Contract/Contract.swift` and the harness's Python models, together)

All new fields optional, so existing fixtures still decode (eng review D10).

- `AgentAction.Kind` gains `purchase`, `cancelPurchase`, `purchaseOutcome` and `addWorkout` (a one-off planned workout; not a recurring routine).
- `AgentAction.purchase: Purchase?`
  - `id` (stable per trigger: `shoes-<shoesBoughtOn>`, `pass-<planItemId>-<rainAt>`), `item` ("Pacer 9", "Day pass"), `merchant` ("Demo store, no charge", "Demo Gym (no charge)"), `amountCents`
  - `status`: `awaitingApproval` | `bought` | `cancelled`
  - `rule`: the mandate line that allowed or held it (`id`, `text`)
  - `noticed`: the trigger sentence ("Your shoes hit 392 miles. Race day is in 9 days.")
  - `evidence: [{ source, passage }]` and `evidenceLive: Bool`
  - `options: [{ name, amountCents, arrives?, lostBecause? }]`, the chosen option first
  - `cancelBy: Date?` (the free-cancel deadline for auto-buys)
  - `outcome: String?` ("Arrived Demo Gym 6:45 PM · pass used", "$15 refunded")
- `AgentAction.purchaseId: String?` on every follow-on action (bought, cancel, outcome, break-in runs), so they fold into one trail.
- `Slot.place: String?` ("Demo Gym"; nil means the plan's usual place). A purchase or cancel that moves a workout carries `before`/`after` slots like a move, so the existing plan replay, the Today row and the calendar entry (its location field) pick up the place.
- `TickSnapshot` gains:
  - `mandate: [{ id, text, category, maxAutoCents }]`: `category` is `dayPass` or `any`; at or under `maxAutoCents` in that category the agent buys without asking; everything else asks first.
  - `approvedPurchaseIds: [String]`
  - `demo: { rainAt: Date?, forecastClear: Bool, shoesBoughtOn: Date?, shoesStartMiles: Int?, raceDate: Date? }`
  - `visits: [{ place, at }]`, recorded by the "Arrive at the gym" demo control at the demo clock's now. The real geofence only shows a notification and runs no app code, so it records nothing.

### How a purchase changes over time (fixes the "phone ignores known ids" rule)

The phone keeps ignoring action ids it already has. Every state change is therefore a **new action with its own stable id**, and the UI folds actions that share a `purchaseId`:

| Event | New action id | Kind | Status after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent asks (shoes) | `purchase-<id>` | `purchase` | `awaitingApproval` |
| Agent auto-buys (pass) | `purchase-<id>` | `purchase` | `bought` |
| Person taps Order it → next tick | `purchase-<id>-bought` | `purchase` | `bought` |
| Break-in runs after an approved order | `breakin-<id>-1`, `breakin-<id>-2` | `addWorkout` | (adds two one-off plan items) |
| Forecast clears before `cancelBy`, or the person's Undo before `cancelBy` | `purchase-<id>-cancel` | `cancelPurchase` | `cancelled` |
| Gym visit inside the workout window | `purchase-<id>-outcome` | `purchaseOutcome` | `bought`, outcome set |

The folded state is the latest status and outcome across the group. The Today card shows the folded purchase while any action in the group is the newest action. Its follow-ons (bought, break-in runs, cancel, outcome) appear as their own rows only in the feed, so the card never jumps to a break-in run. Plan replay skips `addWorkout` actions whose purchase is cancelled or undone.

### Not now and Undo, per row

Each control marks exactly one action id undone, and the rules read these snapshot conditions:

| Control | Marks undone | Rule reads | Agent's response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not now (shoes asking) | `purchase-<id>` | `purchase-<id>` in `undoneActionIds` | Nothing more for that `id`: no bought, no break-in runs, never asks again for it. A newer pair is a new `id`. Feed row reads "Not now". |
| Order it (shoes asking) | nothing; appends the Purchase `id` to `approvedPurchaseIds` and ticks | `id` in `approvedPurchaseIds` and `purchase-<id>` not undone | Emits `purchase-<id>-bought` and the two break-in runs. |
| Cancel order (shoes bought) | `purchase-<id>-bought` | `purchase-<id>-bought` in `undoneActionIds` | Emits `purchase-<id>-cancel` ("Order cancelled, no charge"); replay drops the break-in runs; never re-approves. |
| Undo (day pass, before `cancelBy`) | `purchase-<id>` | `purchase-<id>` in `undoneActionIds` and `now` < `cancelBy` | Emits `purchase-<id>-cancel` ("You cancelled it. $15 refunded") with the workout moved back; never re-buys for that `id`. |

After `cancelBy` the day pass hides Undo and the row reads "Free cancel ended 5:30 PM".

Purchase-group rows show only the controls in this table: the feed's generic Undo (FeedRow) is hidden on every row with a purchaseId, so the asking row has no Undo once Order it or Not now has been used.

- **The agent's own cancel row and outcome rows:** no Undo.
- **Break-in run rows:** Undo removes that run, as for any planned workout today.

### Rules (Swift stand-in and harness `decide()`, the same rules in both)

All times use the snapshot's `now`, which is the demo clock when it's set.

**Shoes (ask-first branch, Jordan):**
- Pair mileage = `shoesStartMiles` + (run minutes since `shoesBoughtOn`) ÷ 9 min/mi, a fixed pace because workouts carry no distance. Jordan has ~1,125 run minutes in the 4-week history ≈ 125 mi, so the demo control sets `shoesBoughtOn` to his history start and `shoesStartMiles` = 267, and the total reads 392.
- Trigger when mileage ≥ 375 and `raceDate` is within 14 days. The demo control sets `raceDate` = story day + 9, overriding the mock data's Dec 6.
- Dates, with the story day Fri Nov 20: race Sun Nov 29; Pacer 9 arrives Mon Nov 23; Glide Trainer arrives Fri Nov 27; Tempo Lite arrives Tue Nov 24.
- Keep options that arrive at least 3 days before the race and match his fit notes; choose the cheapest of those. Pacer 9 ($138) is the only one left: Glide Trainer arrives 2 days before the race; Tempo Lite has a narrow toe box. Each loser gets its `lostBecause`.
- $138 is over every auto-buy line → `awaitingApproval`. After approval: `bought`, then two `addWorkout` break-in runs between arrival and race − 2 days: Tue Nov 24 6:00 PM 30 min and Thu Nov 26 7:00 AM 25 min.

**Day pass (auto-buy branch, Sam):**
- An outdoor workout (Walking, Running or Cycling, including a comeback) matches when `rainAt` is within [start − 30 min, end].
- The `dayPass` mandate allows ≤ $20 → buy the $15 Demo Gym pass (`bought`), move the workout to `place: "Demo Gym"`, `cancelBy` = start − 60 min.
- `forecastClear` before `cancelBy` → `cancelPurchase` ("Forecast cleared for 6:30 PM. $15 refunded; your walk is back outside.") and the workout moves back.
- A visit to Demo Gym within [start − 30 min, end] → `purchaseOutcome` ("Arrived Demo Gym 6:45 PM · pass used").

### Senso content (Tony) and call (Surya)

- Knowledge base: a running-shoe care guide passage (300–500 mile replacement), Jordan's fit notes ("wide toe box, no carbon plate"), three fictional listings with prices, arrival dates and return policies (as above), and the Demo Gym day-pass policy ($15, free cancel until 60 min before).
- The harness calls Senso's context search for each purchase and returns the passages as `evidence` with `evidenceLive: true`; on error or timeout it returns the cached passages with `evidenceLive: false`.
- The same passages are bundled in the app for the stand-in.

### Screens (Anna: visual pass on DESIGN.md tokens)

- **Today card, purchase variant:** verb "Picked new running shoes" / "Bought gym day pass", the noticed line, before → after, a "$138 · asks first" or "$15 · under your $20 rule" badge, a "Demo store, no charge" badge, the keep line, and Order it + Why this pair + Not now (ask-first) or Why + Undo (auto-buy).
- **Why screen (new):** a vertical trail: Your rule → What I noticed → What I checked (citations, live/cached marker) → Options (chosen first, others with why they lost) → What I did / will do → Outcome.
- **Feed rows:** purchase, bought, cancel, outcome and break-in rows in the existing feed style.
- No rings, red or confetti (DESIGN.md). The accent marks the agent's actions as everywhere else.

### Demo controls (added to the existing Demo sheet)

A persona switch (and so both presets) now also clears `approvedPurchaseIds`, the `demo` conditions and `visits`, on top of what it clears today (actions, logged workouts, the clock, completions, the last response). Each preset then sets its own values:

- **"Jordan: shoes near 400 mi":** switches to Jordan and sets `shoesBoughtOn`, `shoesStartMiles` and `raceDate`.
- **"Sam: back to 1:30 PM":** switches to Sam and sets the demo clock to 1:30 PM, so the comeback is re-booked at 6:30 PM.
- **"Rain at 6:30 PM"** sets `rainAt` and `forecastClear = false`; **"Forecast clears"** sets `forecastClear = true`.
- The outcome rule only counts visits at or before `now`.
- **"Arrive at the gym"** (exists): also records a visit at the demo clock's now.
- Reset demo clears purchases, approvals, demo conditions and visits.

### Demo script (opens on a purchase)

Before going on stage, set up Jordan with "Jordan: shoes near 400 mi".

1. **0:00** Jordan's Today: "Picked new running shoes", $138 · asks first. Tap Why this pair: rule, 392 miles, three Senso citations marked live (or cached, if Senso is down), and the two pairs that lost and why.
2. **0:45** Order it. The card shows bought, the feed adds two break-in runs, and the Calendar app shows them next week (scroll to Tue and Thu). Jordan's part ends here: switching persona starts a fresh run.
3. **1:15** "Sam: back to 1:30 PM". The missed 12:00 walk already has a 6:30 PM comeback. Rain at 6:30 PM: the agent buys a $15 day pass under the $20 rule and moves the comeback to Demo Gym.
4. **1:45** Forecast clears: the agent cancels its own pass, "$15 refunded", and the walk goes back outside. Rehearsed alternative instead of step 4: set the clock to 6:45 PM and tap Arrive at the gym → "Arrived Demo Gym 6:45 PM · pass used".
5. **2:15** Close on Sam's feed: the pass traces from rule to outcome. Pitch line.
