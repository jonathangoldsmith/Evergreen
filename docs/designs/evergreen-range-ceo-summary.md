## Vision

### 10x Check
Every flag and its outcome (swap accepted or kept, hurt or not two weeks later) becomes a labeled data point that no plan-making app collects: injury risk measured on top of any plan or coach. The accepted scope more than triples the effort, from about 4 human weeks to 16–19 after the eng review (CC ~3.5–4 weeks). That's about 7 calendar weeks for four people, because milestone 1 is mostly sequential and harness contract changes go through one person. In return, Evergreen becomes the coach's safety net and the newcomer's social push. Vision only, not in scope: an API that lets other training apps plug into the guardrail.

### Platonic Ideal
You rarely open Evergreen. Each morning one tap ("Fresh / Tired / Sore") on your watch. When your plan asks too much it says so, with a smaller run ready; your coach sees what changed and why. After an injury it walks you back to running. On Sunday you learn you stayed in range 5 of 6 days. For a newcomer it works the other way: a missed day becomes a 15-minute walk that evening, and a friend notices when it's done.
